After Encounter 10/20/30 Days Followup

Structured check-ins to support your recovery after care.

Recovery does not end at discharge. StartHere provides scheduled follow-up advocacy at 10, 20, and 30 days after your encounter to monitor progress, address concerns, and keep your care on track.

Followup Schedule

  • 10-Day Check-In – Review how you are feeling, confirm medications are working, and address early recovery questions.
  • 20-Day Check-In – Assess progress on follow-up appointments, therapy, and any lingering symptoms.
  • 30-Day Check-In – Evaluate overall recovery, identify ongoing needs, and plan next steps if additional support is required.

What We Monitor

  • Symptom changes and medication adherence
  • Completion of follow-up appointments
  • Barriers to care (transportation, insurance, home support)
  • Questions or concerns that arise during recovery
