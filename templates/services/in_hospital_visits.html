In-Hospital Patient Visits

Consistent bedside advocacy throughout your hospital stay.

Hospital stays often involve multiple providers, shifting care plans, and important decisions. Our advocates visit you in the hospital to ensure continuity, clarity, and confidence in your care.

What We Do

  • Regular in-person visits at your bedside
  • Attend rounds and key conversations with your care team when appropriate
  • Help you understand diagnoses, treatments, and options
  • Track medications, procedures, and scheduled tests
  • Identify and address gaps in communication or care coordination

Benefits for Patients & Families

Having a dedicated advocate reduces confusion, improves communication with providers, and gives families peace of mind knowing someone experienced is monitoring the details of your hospital stay.
