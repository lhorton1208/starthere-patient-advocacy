Discharge Support – Patient Advocacy

Clear guidance for a safe transition home.

Leaving the hospital is a critical transition. Discharge instructions can be complex and easy to miss. Our advocates help you understand your plan, organize follow-up care, and leave with confidence.

What We Do

  • Review discharge instructions with you and your family
  • Clarify medication changes, dosages, and schedules
  • Confirm follow-up appointments and specialist referrals
  • Identify equipment, home care, or therapy needs
  • Provide a written summary of key action items

Why Discharge Support Matters

Many readmissions and complications happen because discharge plans are unclear or incomplete. StartHere advocates bridge that gap so you know exactly what to do after you leave the hospital.
