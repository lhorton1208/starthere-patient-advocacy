ER Admittance – Patient Advocate

Immediate advocacy when every minute matters.

Emergency room visits are stressful and fast-paced. A StartHere patient advocate can meet you at the ER to help communicate with staff, track test results, and ensure your concerns are heard from the moment you arrive.

What We Do

  • Meet you or your family member at the emergency department
  • Help communicate symptoms, history, and questions to the care team
  • Track orders, test results, and wait times
  • Explain procedures and next steps in plain language
  • Coordinate with family members and keep everyone informed

When to Request an Advocate

Consider requesting ER admittance advocacy if you are managing a complex condition, feel overwhelmed, need help communicating with providers, or want a trusted professional by your side during a critical moment.
