About StartHere

Meet the leaders behind StartHere — combining clinical experience, healthcare technology expertise, and compassionate patient advocacy.

Mission

To provide compassionate, professional advocacy and care coordination services that empower seniors and support families through every stage of healthcare and aging-related transitions.

StartHere Patient Advocacy is an independent advocacy practice. We are not a medical provider and do not offer clinical diagnoses, treatment, or nursing services.

Vision

To become the Triangle's most trusted resource for senior healthcare navigation, advocacy, and aging-related support. Where every patient and family feels informed, empowered, and never alone.
